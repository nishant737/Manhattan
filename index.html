Manhattan Residences: Luxury Apartments in Hampankatta, Mangalore

A project by Mohtisham & Allegro. Luxury elevated in the heart of Mangalore.

About Manhattan

Manhattan is a luxury residential tower in Hampankatta, Mangalore's Central Business District. Conceived by a Dubai-based team of architects, it rises with sculpted balconies and a lit facade, with double-height ceilings, open-plan living, imported stone finishes and rooftop lounges with panoramic city views.
